**Promotional text (170):**

```
All 150 psalms of the 1650 Scottish Psalter, with a choice of tunes. The player plays each tune in four parts and lights each syllable as you sing.
```

**Description (4000):**

```
Precentor helps you sing the Psalms of David in Metre, the Scottish Psalter of 1650.

"Speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord." (Ephesians 5:19)

In Scottish churches the precentor led the singing of the psalms, one line at a time. This app does the same.

• All 150 psalms, and the second versions of 13 psalms
• A choice of tunes for each psalm, in the same metre
• The player plays the tune in four parts
• Each syllable lights up when you must sing it
• The music for each tune
• Links to the notes of the Self-Interpreting Bible on each psalm

The psalms and the tunes are in the app, so it works with no connection. It plays when your iPhone is on silent.

Precentor is free. It has no account, no advertisements and no tracking. Its code is open source.
```

**What's new (version 1):**

```
The first release on the App Store.
```

**App Privacy:** select "Data Not Collected". The native app has no analytics and no account.

**Notes for the reviewer:**

```
No account is needed. Open a psalm and tap Play. Choose another tune from the tune list of the psalm. The Music tab shows the score. Audio plays through the speaker when the silent switch is on, because the app uses the playback audio session.
```
